Stop any movement and call your provider for bleeding, fluid leakage, regular contractions, dizziness, chest pain, calf swelling (especially one-sided), decreased baby movement, or severe headache/visual changes. **Ball safety (unchanged):** inflate firm — hips slightly *higher* than knees when seated. Barefoot on a non-slip floor, on and off slowly from a braced kneel or the edge of the bed. Gentle and rhythmic, never jarring. And **never while breathless, light-headed, or alone in the house at 3 AM** — the ball is a comfort tool now, not equipment.

**What "the holding pattern" means.** Week 38 retired the circuit; week 39 trims the *frequency* of what's left. This is not a concession to fatigue — it's the taper's last stage, and the taper exists because a body that arrives at labour rested labours better than one that arrives fit. **A day with no movement and two naps remains a perfect week-39 day.** The only item that keeps its daily dose is the perineal massage, because tissue preparation is the one thing that can't be caught up on later.

**🌡️ Heat note (Singapore):** cool hours only — early morning or after sundown, water carried, and treat swelling or flushing as a stop signal rather than something to walk off. In the last fortnight, heat is the most common reason a walk becomes a bad idea halfway through.

## 🚶‍♀️ 1. Walking — 20 Minutes, Gentle, Or Not At All

- Conversational pace, no distance goal, no time goal beyond "about twenty minutes."
- **Split it freely**: two 10-minute walks at either end of the day often work better than one 20 at 39 weeks, especially in the heat.
- Pelvic pressure, back ache, or a sudden need to sit → stop, sit, drink. That's the whole protocol. No "push through to finish the loop."
- **Curb walking** a few minutes each side only if it feels good — asymmetric pelvis work as comfort, never as assignment. It has no labour-starting power ([`reading.md`](reading.md) §4 and [`../week-38/reading.md`](../week-38/reading.md) §5).
- **Skip it entirely** on a bad-sleep day. Sleep beats walking every single time now, and no evidence says otherwise.

**The one caveat that replaces everything else:** if contractions start during a walk, the walk ends. Note the time, go home, rest, and time them without walking them faster — early labour is better spent lying down than striding ([`../week-37/reading.md`](../week-37/reading.md) §4).

## 🩷 2. The Ball Block + Hip Openers — 10 Minutes, 2–3 Days

Ten minutes, on the days the body wants it, with comfort leading:

- **Circles** — 2 min each direction, slow and small
- **Figure-8s** — 2 min, loose and unhurried
- **Gentle bouncing** — 2 min, tiny amplitude, jaw loose; if it feels jarring, skip it and sit still instead
- **Lean-over rest** — 2 min over the ball or the bed, forearms down, back long, breath slow
- **Release breathing on the ball** — 2 min, the same pelvic floor release practiced everywhere else

**Hip openers, as comfortable:** hip circles standing, a supported gentle squat, a wide-knee child's pose, a seated butterfly. No holds to beat, no seconds to add. **The deep squat is the mantra slot**, not a strength exercise now — exhale "oooooh," jaw loose, one chosen phrase, then come up. Twenty seconds done well beats ninety seconds of gritting.

**Jaw-loose practice continues inside everything.** Tight jaw = tight pelvic floor; the micro-habit is automatic by now, but keep checking it deliberately. It's still the cheapest high-value skill in the program, and it's about to be cashed in.

**Nothing is added this week.** No new stretch, no "turning" routine, no internet protocol, no prop bought in hope. That was week 37's line and it holds to the end.

## 🩷 3. The Perineal Prep Block — 5 Minutes, Daily

The only daily item left. Full protocol, evidence and contraindications are in the Week 34 guide: [`../week-34/reading.md`](../week-34/reading.md) §§3–4.

1. **Prep:** bladder empty, hands washed, nails short, warm shower or warm compress first. Oil: sweet almond, coconut, vitamin E, or a water-soluble lubricant.
2. **Position:** semi-reclined with knees bent and apart on pillows, one foot on the bath edge, or side-lying. A mirror helps placement.
3. **Technique:** thumbs or fingers 1–1.5 inches (2–4 cm) inside, pressing *down toward the rectum* and *out to the sides* — the direction the head travels. Slow **U-shape** sweep, 6 o'clock → 3 o'clock → 9 o'clock and back, **holding each end 60–90 seconds**. Two to four minutes of actual massage per session.
4. **Breathe under the stretch:** exhale, drop the jaw, let the pelvic floor melt *against* the pressure. Stretch + release is the skill — not depth.
5. **Target sensation:** stretching, pressure, mild burning. **Stop for sharp pain, a lingering sting, or any bleeding.**

**If a sweep is done at the weekly visit** ([`reading.md`](reading.md) §4), skip the massage for 24 hours afterwards and let the spotting settle. Nothing is lost — the tissue work over the last five weeks is already banked.

> ⛔ **Hard stops (unchanged):** placenta praevia, active vaginal infection or herpes outbreak, unexplained bleeding or fluid leak, or once waters have broken. Call the provider rather than pushing on. **Anything vaginal stops after waters break.**

## 🧠 4. The Mental Lineup

- **HypnoBirthing: 30 min daily** — maintained, not increased. Consistency over depth; the tracks are familiar by now, which is the whole point.
- **Visualization: 15 min daily — the chest moment.** This week's slot: build the dim room, then rehearse the *end* — the last push, the wet weight of the baby, skin-to-skin on her chest, the smell of the top of a newborn's head, the first cry and then the little snuffles, and the quiet feeling of *oh, it's you.* Full guidance in [`reading.md`](reading.md) §6. Keep the week-38 wave imagery available for the hard middle; spend this week's minutes past it.
- **Release meditation: 10 min daily** — the script in [`reading.md`](reading.md) §5, read aloud by him or recorded in his voice. Daily, not as a 3 AM rescue.
- **Surrender practice** — now folded into the release meditation; no separate slot needed.
- **Affirmations out loud, once a day:** *"I trust the timing. I trust my body. I trust my baby."*
- **Vocalization 5 min** — the low "oooooh" inside the squat or the ball block, with the mantras.

**Mental load check:** the two slots above (15 + 10) plus HypnoBirthing replace nothing new — they *are* the mental work, and if a day only fits one of them, the release meditation is the one to keep. It's the one that answers the fortnight.

## 🧭 6. One Note on the Body's Timing

At 39 weeks the body is doing things she can't see: the cervix softening, the baby settling lower (which is why the bladder is a rumour and the pelvis feels heavy), the Braxton-Hicks getting stronger and more regular. **None of it is a countdown and none of it can be accelerated by effort.** The gentle movement in this file keeps her comfortable and mobile; it does not bring labour on, and that is fine — [`reading.md`](reading.md) §4 has the honest evidence, and §2 has the reframe.

If the head's position has been on her mind since 10 Oct, ask at the weekly visit whether it's shifted. Head-down is head-down; the plan doesn't move either way, and there is still no corrective routine — no inversions, no internet turning protocols, no chiropractor booked in hope. That was week 37's line and it stands to the end.
