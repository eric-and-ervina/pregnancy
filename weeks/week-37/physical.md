# Week 37 Exercise & Yoga Guide — The Deep Taper: Four Gentle Days, Rest as the Program

> **Where you are:** Week 37 (11 Oct – 17 Oct 2026) · 37w 0d → 37w 6d (D259–265) · **early term** · ~21 → 15 days to EDD (1 Nov)
> **Program reference:** [`PROGRAM.md`](../../program/PROGRAM.md) Week 37 — Phase 4 "Final Countdown" · [`THIRD-TRIMESTER-GUIDE.md`](../../program/THIRD-TRIMESTER-GUIDE.md)
> **👶 Baby:** ~6.5 lb / 2.9 kg — head down (per the 10 Oct visit), face toward her right side: a transverse head position that most babies rotate out of on their own, often *during* labour. Nothing to fix — see §8.
> **🔽 New this week:** the **Birth Prep Circuit drops to 4×/week**, walking to **20–30 gentle minutes**, and the week's only real instruction is a subtraction: **nothing in this file should feel like effort.** The taper that began at week 36 deepens — rest stops being "scheduled alongside" the program and becomes the program itself. The mental rehearsal that pairs with this file is [`reading.md`](reading.md) — Session 14, the Birth Day Playbook.
> **Safety first:** Clear this routine with your OB. Stop any movement and call your provider for bleeding, fluid leakage, regular contractions, dizziness, chest pain, calf swelling (especially one-sided), decreased baby movement, or severe headache/visual changes. Never lie flat on your back for long periods. Breathe continuously — exhale on effort, never hold your breath. The Forward-Leaning Inversion is off the menu if you have high blood pressure, significant heartburn, or your provider has flagged baby's position for special handling. **Perineal massage stops for placenta previa, active vaginal infection or herpes outbreak, unexplained bleeding or a fluid leak, or once waters have broken** — see the ⚠️ When to Call, Not Read section of [`reading.md`](reading.md).

**Ball safety (unchanged):** inflate firm — hips slightly *higher* than knees when seated, thighs roughly level. Barefoot on a non-slip floor, on and off slowly from a braced kneel or the edge of the bed. Gentle and rhythmic, never jarring.

**What "deep taper" means.** Week 36's line was *maintain, don't gain*. Week 37's is shorter: **rest is the training.** Every session below is optional in the way a good habit is optional — do it because it feels good, skip it the moment it doesn't. A skipped session this week is not a gap in the program; a skipped nap is.

**🌡️ Heat note (Singapore):** cool-hours rule still non-negotiable. Walk early morning or after sundown, carry water, treat swelling or flushing as a stop signal.

## 🚶‍♀️ 1. Daily Walking — 20–30 Minutes, Gentle

Conversational pace, shorter ceiling than week 36:

- Warm up 5 min easy · middle 10–20 min easy · cool down 5 min easy
- **No distance goals, no time goals.** Pelvic pressure or back discomfort → split into two 10–15-minute walks or stop at 20 minutes. The taper explicitly permits all of it.
- Cool hours, water carried. Curb walking stays inside the circuit (a few minutes each side) — the asymmetric pelvis work continues because it gives the baby room: head-down with the face to the right is a position that room helps (see §8).

## ⭐ 2. The Birth Prep Circuit — 4×/Week, Gentle (Same Seven Steps, Softer Still)

The seven steps in the same order — the mobility edition, one notch softer than week 36. If any step feels like effort rather than movement, soften it further or skip to the next one.

| # | Step | Week 37 dose | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cat-cow** | 6–8 slow rounds | Mobilizes the spine; a gentle start |
| 2 | **Deep squat hold** | **1–2 min** (or as comfortable) | The mantra slot — exhale "oooooh," jaw loose, say the chosen phrase once. "As comfortable" outranks the clock |
| 3 | **Forward-Leaning Inversion** | 30 sec × 2, optional | Never on a full stomach, never if dizzy; rise via kneeling. Skip freely — the other six stand alone |
| 4 | **Curb walking** | 3 min each side | Asymmetric pelvic opening — the step that gives a transverse-positioned head room to rotate |
| 5 | **Birth ball hip circles** | 60–90 sec each direction | Sitting tall, knees lower than hips; circles then figure-8s |
| 6 | **Pelvic floor release breathing** | **5 min** | The dose holds — release on demand remains the signature skill |
| 7 | **Child's pose** | 60–90 sec | Close the circuit; decompress the lower back |

**Taper refinements:**
- **Jaw-loose practice continues inside every stretch and squat.** Tight jaw = tight pelvic floor — the micro-habit is now automatic; keep checking it deliberately.
- **The add-ons continue only if welcome** (~4 min, after step 7): quick flicks ×10, lunges with pelvic tilt 10/side, hands-and-knees rocking 2 min. If quick flicks cause leaking, heaviness, or bulging — stop the flicks, keep the release work, mention it at the 19 Oct visit.

### The Daily Ball Block — 10–15 min, comfort-led

Circles · figure-8s · gentle bouncing · lean-over rest · release breathing on the ball — same five drills, shorter, and the instruction from week 36 stands: **comfort leads, the clock follows.** If bouncing feels like too much, the whole block becomes lean-over rest + release breathing and that's a complete session. Evening while watching something remains the natural home.

## 🩷 3. The Perineal Prep Block — 5 Minutes, Daily

Full protocol, evidence and contraindications are in the Week 34 guide: [`../week-34/reading.md`](../week-34/reading.md) §3–4. Daily 5 minutes continues through to birth.

1. **Prep:** bladder empty, hands washed, nails short, a warm shower or warm compress first. Oil: sweet almond, coconut, vitamin E, or a water-soluble lubricant.
2. **Position:** semi-reclined with knees bent and apart on pillows, one foot on the bath edge, or side-lying. A mirror helps placement.
3. **Technique:** thumbs or fingers 1–1.5 inches (2–4 cm) inside, pressing *down toward the rectum* and *out to the sides* — the direction the head will travel. Slow **U-shape** sweep, 6 o'clock → 3 o'clock → 9 o'clock and back, **holding each end for 60–90 seconds**. Two to four minutes of actual massage per session.
4. **Breathe under the stretch:** exhale, drop the jaw, let the pelvic floor melt *against* the pressure. Stretch + release is the skill — not depth.
5. **Target sensation:** stretching, pressure, mild burning. **Stop for sharp pain, a lingering sting, or any bleeding.**

**Sustainability:** anchored to the evening shower or bedtime — same time, same place, no decision required. A 2-minute gentle stretch-and-release on a wrecked day still counts as "daily."

> ⛔ **Hard stops (unchanged):** placenta previa, active vaginal infection or herpes outbreak, unexplained bleeding or fluid leak, or once waters have broken. Call the provider rather than pushing on.

## 🧘 4. Gentle Yoga 3× + Daily Stretching (Days 2, 5, 7)

Three short sessions, ~15–20 minutes each — hip-opening, floor-based, breathing-led. No new poses; the sequence stands, shortened again:

1. **Cat-cow** — 6 rounds, slow
2. **Malasana / yogi squat** — supported against the wall or over a rolled blanket, 60 sec *(jaw loose)*
3. **Baddha konasana / butterfly** — 90 sec, knees supported, gentle forward fold
4. **Kneeling hip flexor lunge** — 45 sec each side, back knee on a cushion
5. **Happy baby** — 60 sec, holding behind the thighs
6. **Side-lying with pillows** — 3 min of pelvic floor release breathing to close

**Daily stretch on non-yoga days (~10 min):** kneeling hip flexor lunge 45 sec/side · hamstring stretch 45 sec/side · butterfly 60 sec · chest opener 30 sec × 2 — every hold a jaw-loose check.

**Water session (1× if accessible):** swimming or water walking, 20–30 minutes — still the kindest session in the program. Counts fully.

## 👶 8. A Note on the Baby's Position at 37 Weeks

Saturday's visit confirmed: head down, face toward her right side. In plain terms — the important part (head-down) is in place; the head is turned sideways rather than tucked face-down toward her spine.

Three things worth knowing, none of them alarming:

1. **This is common at 37 weeks.** A large share of babies are still in transverse or posterior head positions now and rotate during labour itself — the rotation is often what the early hours are *doing*.
2. **There is nothing to fix and no corrective routine to add.** The program's existing asymmetric work — curb walking, hands-and-knees rocking, the deep squat — is exactly the "give the baby room" prescription. Aggressive turning protocols from the internet trade anxiety for effort; skip them.
3. **Mention it at the 19 Oct visit** (~38w 1d) if you want the doctor's read on whether it's shifted. The birth plan doesn't change either way — head-down is head-down.

## ⚠️ What to Avoid This Week

- **Treating the deep taper as laziness and "making up" sessions** — the reduced volume *is* the program
- **Pushing walking distance or time** — 20–30 gentle minutes is the ceiling, not the floor
- **Adding any new exercise, stretch, or "turning routine"** — nothing new enters the body in the last month. Maintain, don't gain
- **Skipping rest days' naps** — sleep is the #1 priority now
- **Perineal massage through a contraindication** — previa, infection/herpes outbreak, bleeding, fluid leak, or ruptured membranes. Call first
- **Anything vaginal (EPO, massage) after waters break**
- **Exhausting herself with nesting** — light organizing only
- **FLI on a full stomach, with dizziness/heartburn, or with flagged position** — skip it; the rest runs fine without it
- **Ball bouncing while breathless or queasy, or mounting a rolling ball**
- **Quick flicks if they cause leaking or heaviness** — stop the flicks, keep the release work, mention it at the visit
- **Holding your breath** anywhere — exhale on effort
- **Flat-on-back work** — side-lying and tilted positions only
- **Ignoring the call-now list** — water breaking (time + colour), bright red bleeding, reduced movement, severe headache/visual changes: call, don't wait

## 🌟 The Bottom Line

Week 37 is the week the program gets out of the way. Four gentle circuit days, short walks, the same daily perineal five minutes — and underneath it, the real work is rest and rehearsal: the birth walked through end-to-end in the reading, the body kept supple and calm in the movement. Early term means the waiting can end any day now, and that is *fine* — everything that could be prepared has been prepared. The bag is by the door, the card is in it, the partner's hands know what to do, and the calendar holds nothing but naps. From here, the baby sets the schedule.
