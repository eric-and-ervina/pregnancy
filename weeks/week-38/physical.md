## 🩷 2. The Gentle Movement Menu — As Comfortable

No order, no sets, no completion criteria. When the body asks for movement:

- **Cat-cow** — 6–8 slow rounds, breathing into the movement
- **Child's pose** — 60–90 seconds, knees wide, forehead down, lower back melting
- **Hip circles** — standing or on the ball, 60–90 seconds each direction, then figure-8s
- **Birth ball block** — up to 10 minutes: circles, gentle bouncing, lean-over rest, release breathing on the ball. If bouncing feels like too much, the whole block becomes lean-over rest + release breathing and that's complete
- **Deep squat** — as comfortable, supported against the wall or over a rolled blanket if wanted; exhale "oooooh," jaw loose, the chosen phrase once
- **Pelvic floor release breathing** — 5 minutes, the dose that holds to the end

**Jaw-loose practice continues inside every stretch and squat.** Tight jaw = tight pelvic floor — the micro-habit is automatic now; keep checking it deliberately. It's the cheapest high-value skill in the program and it pays out in labour.

**The add-ons are retired with the circuit.** Quick flicks, lunges, hands-and-knees rocking as drills — done. If she finds herself rocking on hands and knees because it feels good, that's comfort, not a drill, and it's welcome.

## 🩷 3. The Perineal Prep Block — 5 Minutes, Daily

Full protocol, evidence and contraindications are in the Week 34 guide: [`../week-34/reading.md`](../week-34/reading.md) §3–4. Daily 5 minutes continues through to birth — this is the one physical item that stays *daily* rather than *as comfortable*.

1. **Prep:** bladder empty, hands washed, nails short, a warm shower or warm compress first. Oil: sweet almond, coconut, vitamin E, or a water-soluble lubricant.
2. **Position:** semi-reclined with knees bent and apart on pillows, one foot on the bath edge, or side-lying. A mirror helps placement.
3. **Technique:** thumbs or fingers 1–1.5 inches (2–4 cm) inside, pressing *down toward the rectum* and *out to the sides* — the direction the head will travel. Slow **U-shape** sweep, 6 o'clock → 3 o'clock → 9 o'clock and back, **holding each end for 60–90 seconds**. Two to four minutes of actual massage per session.
4. **Breathe under the stretch:** exhale, drop the jaw, let the pelvic floor melt *against* the pressure. Stretch + release is the skill — not depth.
5. **Target sensation:** stretching, pressure, mild burning. **Stop for sharp pain, a lingering sting, or any bleeding.**

**Sustainability:** anchored to the evening shower or bedtime — same time, same place, no decision required. A 2-minute gentle stretch-and-release on a wrecked day still counts as "daily."

> ⛔ **Hard stops (unchanged):** placenta previa, active vaginal infection or herpes outbreak, unexplained bleeding or fluid leak, or once waters have broken. Call the provider rather than pushing on. **Anything vaginal stops after waters break.**

## 🧠 4. The Mental Lineup

- **HypnoBirthing: 30 min daily** — maintained, not increased. Consistency over depth.
- **Wave-breathing visualization: 15 min daily** — this week's visualization slot: each contraction as an ocean wave that builds, peaks, breaks, and recedes — you surf it, you don't fight it — with the vivid reel (dim room, his hand, the descent, the first cry, skin-to-skin) folded into the same 15 minutes. Full script in [`reading.md`](reading.md) §9. One slot, not two.
- **Surrender practice: 10 min** — release the need to control timing. Baby comes when ready.
- **Affirmations:** *"My body is ready. My baby is ready. We will meet soon."* — said out loud once a day.
- **Vocalization: 5 min** — low "oooooh" with the mantras, inside the squat or the ball block.

## ⚠️ What to Avoid This Week

- **Treating "gentle movement only" as laziness and reinstating the circuit** — the retired circuit stays retired
- **Pushing walking distance or time** — 20–30 gentle minutes is the ceiling, and skipping is permitted
- **Adding any new exercise, stretch, supplement, or "turning routine"** — nothing new enters the body in the last month. Maintain, don't gain
- **Skipping naps to "stay productive"** — sleep is the #1 priority now
- **Perineal massage or anything vaginal through a contraindication or after waters break** — call first
- **Exhausting herself with nesting** — light organizing only
- **Ball bouncing while breathless or queasy, or mounting a rolling ball**
- **Holding your breath** anywhere — exhale on effort
- **Flat-on-back work** — side-lying and tilted positions only
- **Symptom surveillance by either of you** — no "was that a contraction?" hovering ([`reading.md`](reading.md) §6)
- **Ignoring the call-now list** — water breaking (time + colour), bright red bleeding, reduced movement, severe headache/visual changes: call, don't wait
