# Week 36 Exercise & Yoga Guide — The Taper: Less Volume, Same Habits, More Rest

> **Where you are:** Week 36 (04 Oct – 10 Oct 2026) · 36w 0d → 36w 6d (D252–258) · ~28 → 22 days to EDD (1 Nov)
> **Program reference:** [`PROGRAM.md`](../../program/PROGRAM.md) Week 36 — Phase 3 "Intensive Preparation," week 4 (final) · **the taper begins** + [`THIRD-TRIMESTER-GUIDE.md`](../../program/THIRD-TRIMESTER-GUIDE.md)
> **👶 Baby:** ~6 lb / 2.75 kg (romaine lettuce) — head typically settling into the pelvis around now in a first pregnancy (*lightening*). Kick counts continue; know her normal pattern.
> **🔽 New this week:** the **Birth Prep Circuit drops to 5×/week at reduced intensity** (mobility focus, no pushing), **perineal massage goes daily**, the **deep squat holds at 2 minutes**, pelvic floor release stretches to **5 minutes**, and a new mental item joins the lineup — the **"letting go" meditation** (10 min). The headline change isn't a movement at all: **rest is now scheduled like a session, and naps count as training.**
> The reading that pairs with this file is [`reading.md`](reading.md) — the honest evidence review (Session 13): dates, raspberry leaf, EPO, and what actually prepares the cervix.

**Safety first:** Clear this routine with your OB/midwife. Stop any movement and call your provider if you have bleeding, fluid leakage, regular contractions before 37 weeks, dizziness, chest pain, calf swelling (especially one-sided), decreased baby movement, or severe headache/visual changes. Never lie flat on your back for long periods. Breathe continuously — exhale on effort, never hold your breath. The Forward-Leaning Inversion is off the menu if you have high blood pressure, significant heartburn, or your provider has flagged baby's position for special handling. **Perineal massage is not done if you have placenta previa, an active vaginal infection or herpes outbreak, unexplained bleeding or a fluid leak, or once your waters have broken** — see the ⚠️ When to Call, Not Read section of [`reading.md`](reading.md).

**Ball safety (unchanged):** inflate firm — hips slightly *higher* than knees when seated, thighs roughly level. Roll it a hand-width from a wall for the first few sits, barefoot on a non-slip floor, and get on and off slowly from a braced kneel or the edge of the bed. Gentle and rhythmic, never jarring.

**What "taper" means here.** Borrowed from endurance sport: you don't get fitter in the last weeks, you get *fresher*. Every practice you've built since Week 25 is maintained at a lower dose so nothing detrains, while the recovered energy goes into the only account that matters now — sleep, tissue health, and a calm nervous system. A skipped session this week is not a gap; a skipped nap is.

**🌡️ Heat note (Singapore):** at 36 weeks the cool-hours rule is non-negotiable. Walk early morning or after sundown, carry water, and treat swelling or flushing as a stop signal, not a challenge.

## 🚶‍♀️ 1. Daily Walking — 30 Minutes, Gentle

Same conversational pace, shorter ceiling:

- Warm up 5 min easy · middle 20 min easy · cool down 5 min easy
- **No distance goals.** If pelvic pressure or back discomfort flares, split into two 15-minute walks or shorten to 20 minutes without a second thought — the taper explicitly permits it.
- Cool hours, water carried, **curb walking stays inside the circuit** (5 min each side) — the asymmetric pelvis work continues because descent, not turning, is now the job.

## ⭐ 2. The Birth Prep Circuit — 5×/Week, Reduced Intensity (Same Seven Steps, Softer)

The seven steps, in the same order — but this is the **mobility edition**: slower, shallower, breathing-led. If any step feels like effort rather than movement, soften it further or skip to the next one.

| # | Step | Taper dose | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cat-cow** | 8 slow rounds (down from 10) | Mobilizes the spine; a gentle start |
| 2 | **Deep squat hold** | **2 min** (or as comfortable) | The week's explicit line — but "as comfortable" outranks the clock. Wide-knee supported sit if pubic pain flares |
| 3 | **Forward-Leaning Inversion** | 30 sec × 2 (down from 3), optional | Never on a full stomach, never if dizzy; rise via kneeling. Skip freely — the other six stand alone |
| 4 | **Curb walking** | 3–4 min each side (down from 5) | Asymmetric pelvic opening for descent |
| 5 | **Birth ball hip circles** | 90 sec each direction | Sitting tall, knees lower than hips; circles then figure-8s |
| 6 | **Pelvic floor release breathing** | **5 min** (up from 3) | The dose grows as the effort shrinks — release on demand (the "mool bandh" release in [`PROGRAM.md`](../../program/PROGRAM.md)'s words) is the signature skill now |
| 7 | **Child's pose** | 60–90 sec | Close the circuit; decompress the lower back |

**Taper refinements:**
- **The squat stays the mantra slot** — exhale "oooooh," let the floor drop, say the chosen phrase once. At 2 minutes it's a meditation that happens to open the pelvis.
- **Jaw-loose practice starts here:** during every stretch and every squat, keep the jaw loose and the mouth slightly open. **Tight jaw = tight pelvic floor** — the week's new micro-habit, and the cheapest high-value drill in the program.
- **The three add-ons continue** (~4 min, after step 7): quick flicks ×10, lunges with pelvic tilt 10/side, hands-and-knees rocking 2 min. If quick flicks cause leaking, heaviness, or bulging — stop the flicks, keep the release work, mention it at the appointment.

### The Daily Ball Block — 15 min, comfort-led

Same five drills as Week 35 (circles 3 min each way · figure-8s 3 min · gentle bouncing 3 min · lean-over rest 3 min · release breathing on the ball 3 min) — but the *instruction* changes: **comfort leads, the clock follows.** If bouncing feels like too much, the whole block becomes lean-over rest + release breathing and that's a complete session. Evening while watching something remains the natural home.

## 🩷 3. The Perineal Prep Block — 5 Minutes, Daily From This Week

Full protocol, evidence and contraindications are in the Week 34 guide: [`../week-34/reading.md`](../week-34/reading.md) §3–4. This is the final frequency: **every day, 5 minutes.**

1. **Prep:** bladder empty, hands washed, nails short, a warm shower or warm compress first. Oil: sweet almond, coconut, vitamin E, or a water-soluble lubricant. Not petroleum jelly; nothing warming or fragranced.
2. **Position:** semi-reclined with knees bent and apart on pillows, one foot on the bath edge, or side-lying. A mirror helps placement.
3. **Technique:** thumbs or fingers **1–1.5 inches (2–4 cm) inside**, pressing *down toward the rectum* and *out to the sides* — the direction the head will travel. Sweep a slow **U-shape** from 6 o'clock → 3 o'clock → 9 o'clock and back, **holding each end of the U for 60–90 seconds**. Two to four minutes of actual massage per session.
4. **Breathe under the stretch:** exhale, drop the jaw, consciously let the pelvic floor melt *against* the pressure. The coordinated "stretch + release" is the skill — not the depth of the stretch.
5. **Target sensation:** stretching, pressure, mild burning. **Stop for sharp pain, a lingering sting, or any bleeding** — less pressure, more oil, or stop for the day.

**Making daily sustainable:** anchor it to an existing habit — right after the evening shower, or in bed before sleep. Same time, same place, no decision required. If a day is truly wrecked, a 2-minute gentle stretch-and-release still counts as "daily" — the streak matters more than any single session.

> ⛔ **Hard stops (unchanged):** placenta previa, active vaginal infection or herpes outbreak, unexplained bleeding or fluid leak, or once waters have broken. Call the provider rather than pushing on.

## 🧘 4. Gentle Yoga 3× + Daily Stretching (Days 2, 4, 6)

Three gentle sessions, ~20–25 minutes each — hip-opening, floor-based, breathing-led. No new poses; the Week 35 sequence stands, shortened:

1. **Cat-cow** — 6 rounds, slow
2. **Malasana / yogi squat** — supported against the wall or over a rolled blanket, 60 sec *(jaw loose, mouth slightly open)*
3. **Baddha konasana / butterfly** — 90 sec, knees supported, gentle forward fold
4. **Kneeling hip flexor lunge** — 45 sec each side, back knee on a cushion
5. **Happy baby** — 60 sec, holding behind the thighs, lower back flat
6. **Side-lying with pillows** — 3 min of pelvic floor release breathing to close

**Daily 10-minute stretch (non-yoga days):** kneeling hip flexor lunge 45 sec/side · hamstring stretch 45 sec/side · butterfly 60 sec · chest opener 30 sec × 2. Every stretch is now also **jaw-loose practice** — check the jaw at the start of each hold and unclench it deliberately.

**Water session (1× if accessible):** swimming or water walking, 20–30 minutes — the kindest session in the whole program at 36 weeks. Counts fully.

## 🤝 5. Partner Final Coaching Rehearsal (Day 6, ~25 Minutes)

Week 35 ran the full five-round mock labour. This week is the **final rehearsal** — shorter, sharper, and built from the debrief: it re-tests *only* what the two of you flagged as "needs work," plus the non-negotiables.

### Before you start (5 min)

- Read back the **one-sentence debriefs** from Week 35's rehearsal: what worked, what to change. The "what to change" list is today's curriculum.
- Pocket card ([`../week-35/reading.md`](../week-35/reading.md) §11) where he can see it. Numbers in PART 0 confirmed in pen.

### The rehearsal (3 rounds, ~15 min)

1. **Round 1 — the flagged item.** Whatever scored worst last time (the silence round for most partners; the hip squeeze for most hands). One timed 60-second surge, done slowly, done right. Repeat once if it still feels off — this is practice, not performance.
2. **Round 2 — transition drill.** She acts tense (clenched jaw, held breath, "I can't do this"); he coaches the correction in three-word sentences: *"Drop your jaw. Long breath out. I'm right here."* Non-negotiable round — this is the phase the program has rehearsed since Week 33, and it's the last chance to drill it calm.
3. **Round 3 — the silence round.** One full timed surge, he says nothing, hand on her back, breathing with her. If Week 35's silence round was the hardest, this re-run is the most valuable 90 seconds of the week.

### The one-thing wind-down (5 min)

He says the advocacy lines out loud once, slowly — *"Let me check with her." "We need ten minutes." "Is this an emergency, or can we talk it through?"* — and the two of you confirm the **5-1-1 rule** and *your provider's* head-in threshold from this week's visit. Out loud once is what makes it retrievable at 3 a.m.

**After this week, no more rehearsals are scheduled.** The techniques are in his hands; further drilling has diminishing returns against rest. If a third run is wanted, keep it to one round, one evening — then stop.

## 🧠 7. The Mental Lineup (Unchanged Doses + One New Item)

- **HypnoBirthing: 30 min daily** — maintained, not increased. Consistency over depth now.
- **Birth visualization: daily** — the full rehearsal visualization from Week 35, transition-mindset drill folded inside it if wanted.
- **"Letting go" meditation: 10 min daily (new)** — sit or side-lie, and with each exhale deliberately release one place: jaw, then shoulders, then pelvic floor. Cycle through for ten minutes. This is the nervous-system version of the taper: practising *not holding*.
- **Surrender meditation: 10 min** — continues.
- **Vocalization: 5 min** — low "oooooh" with the mantras, ideally inside the squat or the ball block.

## ⚠️ What to Avoid This Week

- **Treating the taper as laziness and "making up" sessions** — the reduced volume *is* the program. Adding intensity now spends the account labour will draw on
- **Pushing walking distance or time** — 30 gentle minutes is the ceiling, not the floor
- **Skipping rest days' naps** — sleep is a training target, not a leftover
- **Perineal massage through a contraindication** — previa, infection/herpes outbreak, bleeding, fluid leak, or ruptured membranes. Call first
- **Anything vaginal (EPO, massage) after waters break**
- **Scheduling more rehearsals after Day 6** — diminishing returns; rest wins from here
- **New exercises, new stretches, new anything** — nothing new enters the body in the last month. Maintain, don't gain
- **FLI on a full stomach, with dizziness/heartburn, or with flagged position** — skip it; the rest runs fine without it
- **Ball bouncing while breathless or queasy, or mounting a rolling ball**
- **Quick flicks if they cause leaking or heaviness** — stop the flicks, keep the release work, mention it at the appointment
- **Holding your breath** anywhere — exhale on effort, "aaaaah" on every practice surge
- **Flat-on-back work** — side-lying and tilted positions only
- **Ignoring regular contractions before 37 weeks** — regular, timeable contractions, any bleeding, any fluid leak, or any change in movement is a call-now

## 🌟 The Bottom Line

Week 36 is the week the program exhales. The circuit drops to five gentle days, perineal massage reaches its daily dose, and the only new item is a meditation about *letting go* — which tells you everything about where the training has arrived. Nothing about the body changes except that it gets to rest; nothing about the mind changes except that it gets quieter. Underneath the taper sits this week's real work, and it's in the reading: an honest accounting of what prepares the cervix and what doesn't, so the final month is spent on dates and rest instead of castor oil and anxiety. Four weeks out, readiness looks like this: a ripe cervix, a packed bag, a partner whose hands know what to do, and a calendar with nothing left on it but naps.
