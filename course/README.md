# 🎓 The New Dad's Course — From 36 Weeks to the First Month

**For Eric. Read in order, or jump to what you need tonight.**

This is a complete preparation course for the husband and new father: what happens in the final weeks of pregnancy, your job in the labour room at Thomson Medical Centre, what to do when plans change, and how to care for your wife and your newborn son in the weeks after.

Ten chapters, roughly 2.5 hours cover to cover — or 20 minutes for the "tonight" version below.

> This course is education, not medical advice. Your gynae, midwife, and paediatrician always have the final say.
